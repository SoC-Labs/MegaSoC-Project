\chapter{Pin Outs} \label{pinout}
MegaSoC has 2 GPIO ports, P0 and P1. P0 is a completely general purpose with no
alternative functionality. P1 can be used for some peripherals, UART0, UART1, SPI and EXTIO.
The mapping for P1 is as follows

\begin{center}
    \begin{tabular}{||c | c ||}
        \hline
        P1 Pin & Alt Function \\ 
        \hline\hline
        0 & UART0 Rx \\
        1 & UART0 Tx \\
        2 & UART1 Rx \\
        3 & UART1 Tx \\
        4 & SPI SSn \\
        5 & SPI SCLK \\
        6 & SPI MOSI \\
        7 & SPI MISO \\
        8 & EXTIO Data 0 \\
        9 & EXTIO Data 1 \\
        10 & EXTIO Data 2 \\
        11 & EXTIO Data 3 \\
        12 & EXTIO Req 1 \\
        13 & EXTIO Req 2 \\
        14 & EXTIO Ack \\
        15 & N/A \\
        \hline
    \end{tabular}
\end{center}
